# RubberStress User Guide

Welcome to **RubberStress**, the web platform for constitutive modeling of elastomer strain energy density functions.

## Getting Started

1. **Log in or Register**: Access the platform dashboard.
2. **Upload Experimental Data**: Upload CSV files containing stretch ($\lambda$) and nominal stress ($\mathbf{P}$) or Cauchy stress ($\boldsymbol{\sigma}$) for uniaxial, equibiaxial, or pure shear deformation modes.
3. **Select CANN Architecture**: Choose between standard models (Neo-Hookean, Mooney-Rivlin, Ogden, Yeoh, Arruda-Boyce) or CANN architectures (Model A/B/C, ICNN).
4. **Run Optimization**: Submit job to model fitting queue.
5. **Analyze Results**: Review goodness-of-fit plots, parameter estimates, and download exportable reports (PDF / CSV).
